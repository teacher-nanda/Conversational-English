Questions and short answers

Yes / No questions (?)Short answers

Are you OK?Yes, I am.   No, I'm not.
Are we in Los Angeles?Yes, you are.   No, you're not.
Are you students?Yes, we are.   No, we're not.

Are you from Spain? NOT You are from Spain?
Yes, I am. NOT Yes, I'm.

Grammar

be: I / you / we negative

Complete the sentences in the chart.

Affirmative (+)

I a student. (= I am)
We from England. (= we are)

Negative (–)

I'm a student. (= I am not)
We from England. (= we are not)

💭 Check the meaning

  • I'm not = I am not. Is it I'm not or I amn't? It is I'm not.
    We never say I amn't.
  • We're not and we aren't: same meaning or different? The same meaning: we are not.
